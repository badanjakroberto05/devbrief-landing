How it works

From raw tickets to a client-ready report

Three steps. No new workflow for your team, no login for your client.

Step one

Connect your tools

Read-only OAuth to Jira Cloud and GitHub. We never write anything, never touch your code. Map projects and repos to each client once.

Step two

AI drafts the report

Every ticket and PR is translated into plain business language: what was delivered, why it matters, what's in progress, what's next. Your logo, your colours.

Step three

You review & send

Nothing goes out without your approval. Edit any section, regenerate, then export a branded PDF or a shareable link.
